Frequently Asked Questions

What does Semantic HTML mean?

Semantic HTML means choosing elements based on their meaning, not their appearance.

What is header in Semantic HTML?

Header is a visible introductory content

What does the element nav do?

Nav element marks a block of major navigation links.

What does the element main do?

The main element contains the dominant, unique content of the page - the other page

What is footer in Semantic HTML?

Footer is information about the parent: copyright, author, links.
