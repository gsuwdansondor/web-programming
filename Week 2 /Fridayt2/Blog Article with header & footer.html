Why I love Morin Khuur

By UNESCO intangible Cultural Heritage November 7, 2003

The Morin Khuur, also known as the horsehead fiddle, is one of the most important traditional musical instruments of Mongolia. It has two strings and is traditionally decorated with a carved horse's head. The instrument is deeply connected to Mongolian nomadic culture, history, horses, and traditional music.

On November 7, 2003, UNESCO proclaimed the traditional music of the Morin Khuur a Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity. In 2008, it was added to UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. This recognition helped preserve and promote the Morin Khuur as an important part of Mongolia's cultural heritage.
