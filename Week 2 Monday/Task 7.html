How to make Buuz

What You Need

  • 200grams of flour
  • 100 ml of water
  • 150grams of meat
  • salt
  • black pepper (optional)

Steps

  1. Chop the meat into small pieces.
  2. Prepare the flour dough by putting water on the flour.
  3. Leave the dough to rest while seasoning the meat by your own taste.
  4. Prepare the steam pot with hot water.
  5. Place the crimped buuz inside and wait for 15 minutes.
